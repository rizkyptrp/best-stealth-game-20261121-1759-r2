THE QUIET FLOOR

A night infiltration at the Meridian Archive.

You are Wren, a courier with light fingers. Three ledger pages sleep in the archive's vaults, and the night guards walk their rounds with lanterns and long memories. Slip past them, take every page, and leave through the service door — unseen.

MOVE — WASD / Arrow keys
SNEAK — hold Shift (slower, quieter, harder to spot)
THROW A PEBBLE — click the floor (guards investigate the sound)
HIDE — crouch in the bushes; guards cannot see you there unless close
PAUSE — P  ·  RESTART — R  ·  MUTE — M

A guard's cone is what he can see. Fill his suspicion and he raises the alarm — break line of sight and he will search, then give up and go back to his round. Get caught, and the night is over.

3 floors · 2–4 guards each · no one needs to know you were here
